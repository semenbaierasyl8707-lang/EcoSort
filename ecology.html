WASTE CLASSIFICATION

Know Your Waste

Different materials need different recycling methods.

🥤

Plastic

Plastic bottles and containers.

About Plastic Recycling

Plastic is widely used in bottles, packaging and containers. Some types of plastic can be recycled into new products.

How to recycle:

Empty and rinse containers. Check the recycling symbol and local collection rules. Separate recyclable plastic from other waste.

Examples:

Water bottles, food containers, plastic packaging.

📄

Paper

Newspapers, cardboard and paper packaging.

About Paper Recycling

Paper and cardboard can often be processed into new paper products, reducing the need for fresh raw materials.

How to recycle:

Keep paper dry and clean. Flatten cardboard boxes. Remove plastic wrapping and other non-paper materials.

Examples:

Newspapers, notebooks, cardboard boxes, office paper.

🍾

Glass

Glass bottles and jars.

About Glass Recycling

Glass bottles and jars can be recycled into new glass products when accepted by local recycling facilities.

How to recycle:

Empty containers and rinse them. Separate glass from other materials. Handle broken glass carefully and follow local disposal rules.

Examples:

Glass bottles, jars and food containers.

🥫

Metal

Aluminum cans and metal packaging.

About Metal Recycling

Metals such as aluminum and steel can be recycled and used to manufacture new products.

How to recycle:

Empty cans and containers. Rinse away food residue when needed. Place accepted metal items in the correct recycling bin.

Examples:

Aluminum cans, steel food cans and metal packaging.

🍃

Organic

Food scraps and natural waste.

About Organic Waste

Organic waste comes from plants and food. Suitable organic materials can be turned into compost.

How to recycle:

Separate food scraps from other rubbish. Use a suitable compost bin or local organic waste collection service.

Examples:

Fruit peels, vegetable scraps, leaves and coffee grounds.
